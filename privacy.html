FOREVER

Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

The short version. FOREVER stores the training, nutrition and body data you enter so the app can coach you. We don't sell it, we don't use it for ads, and nobody else sees it unless you connect a coach. You can download all of it or delete all of it from inside the app at any time.

Who we are

FOREVER is a bodybuilding coaching app ("FOREVER", "we", "us"). Questions or requests about your data: alwayssimpyn@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, a password, and (if you add them) your name and a profile photo. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and stored only in hashed form — we never see them.
  • What you log: workouts (exercises, weights, reps, effort ratings), training settings and program, food and macros, body weight, tape measurements, sleep, soreness and recovery ratings, steps and cardio, and the goals and diet phase you choose.
  • Progress photos you choose to upload. They are stored privately and shown only through short-lived links.
  • Reminders (optional): if you turn on reminders, your device's push address, your time zone and your reminder settings are stored so we can send notifications. They're deleted when you turn reminders off.
  • Error reports: if the app crashes or you tap "Report a bug", we receive technical details (device and browser type, what went wrong) tagged with your account ID and email so we can fix it.

We don't collect your location, contacts, or anything from other apps. If you use FOREVER without an account ("this device only"), your data stays in your browser on that device and never reaches us.

How we use it

  • To run the app: plan your sets and weights, set calorie and macro targets, track trends and show your history.
  • To sync your data across your devices.
  • To find and fix bugs.

We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it to build profiles for anyone else.

Who else handles it

We rely on a few service providers who process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase — database, file storage for photos, and sign-in (servers in the United States).
  • Sentry — error and crash reporting.
  • Resend — sends account emails such as password resets.
  • Open Food Facts — when you search for a food or scan a barcode, the search words or barcode number are sent to this free public food database. Nothing that identifies you is included.
  • Google (Gemini AI) — only when you use AI food logging: the meal photo or description you choose to send is processed by Google's Gemini AI to estimate calories and macros. Nothing else about you is sent. On the free tier Google may use what's sent to improve its products, so don't use AI logging for photos with people or anything private in them. When we move to a paid AI provider this line will be updated.
  • Google Fonts, our app host (GitHub Pages) and the code libraries we load (jsDelivr, unpkg) — deliver the app's files, fonts and the barcode scanner, which means they see your IP address like any website.

Coaches. If you choose to connect to a coach using their code, that coach can view everything you log in FOREVER — workouts, nutrition, weight, measurements, sleep, soreness and check-ins, steps and cardio, your profile (age, sex, height), your email address, your progress photos, and the form-check videos and voice notes you send them. If your coach runs a challenge, the other clients in it see your username and your challenge total. They can also change your plan: your workout program, your diet phase and maintenance calories, and they can send you notes. They cannot change or delete anything you've logged (workouts, food, weight, measurements, photos). You can disconnect at any time from the menu, and their access ends immediately.

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect someone's safety.

Health information

Body weight, measurements, photos, sleep, soreness and nutrition can be sensitive. We treat all of it as private: it's only used to provide the app to you (and your coach, if you connect one), and it's protected as described below. By entering it you consent to us storing and processing it for that purpose; you can withdraw that consent at any time by deleting your data.

FOREVER gives general training and nutrition guidance. It is not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new training or diet plan, especially if you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or have a history of disordered eating.

How it's protected

  • All data travels encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Database rules limit every account to its own data; a connected coach can read it and edit only your plan (program, diet phase, maintenance calories, and notes to you).
  • Progress photos live in a private storage bucket and are only shown through links that expire within an hour.

No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data and will tell you if a breach affects it.

Your choices and rights

  • Download it: Menu → Download full backup gives you everything in one file. Menu → Export gives your workouts and food as a spreadsheet.
  • Correct it: edit or remove any entry in the app.
  • Delete it: Menu → Delete all my data permanently erases your logs, settings, photos and coach connection. To also remove your login, email us and we'll do it within 30 days.
  • Disconnect a coach at any time from the menu.

Depending on where you live (for example California or Washington State), you may have additional rights to know, access, delete or correct your data, or to withdraw consent. Email us and we'll respond within the time the law requires. We won't treat you differently for using these rights.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists so your history and trends keep working. When you delete your data it is removed from our live systems right away; our providers may keep encrypted backups for a short period before they're overwritten.

Age

FOREVER is for people 16 and older. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger; if you believe a child has created an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll update the date above and let you know in the app before the change takes effect.
