Training

A coach in your pocket, every single set

No spreadsheets, no guessing. Open the app, see today's plan, log the set. FOREVER does the math between sessions.

Progression engine

It tells you exactly what to lift

Every exercise shows today's target weight and reps, based on what you did last time and how hard it felt. Hit the top of your rep range and the weight goes up; have an off day and it holds.

  • Reps-in-reserve targets so every set is hard enough, never reckless
  • A two-tap soreness check-in adjusts next week's sets per muscle
  • Stalls are spotted early and deloads suggested when fatigue builds
  • Machine taken? Swap it for today without breaking your plan

Periodization

Blocks that build, then recover

Training runs in blocks: build volume, overreach, peak, then deload. You see weekly sets per muscle against the range that grows muscle, so nothing gets neglected and nothing gets buried.

  • Block recaps and strength trends for every lift

FAQ

Questions

Is FOREVER in the App Store?

Not yet. FOREVER installs straight from your browser and works like a regular app, with a home-screen icon, full screen and notifications. On iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share › Add to Home Screen. On Android, tap Install in Chrome.

Do I need a coach?

No. FOREVER is built to coach you on its own: it plans your training, progresses your weights and sets your calories. If you work with a coach, they can connect to your account and adjust your plan directly.

Who is it for?

Anyone lifting to build muscle, from your first program to advanced lifters running specialization blocks. The defaults are beginner-friendly; the depth is there when you want it.

Can I bring my MyFitnessPal history?

Yes. Request your data export from MyFitnessPal, then pick the file in Menu › Import history. Your food log and weigh-ins come across, so your trends start with real data.

How does the free trial work?

New accounts get 7 days free with no card required. To keep going after that, pick monthly or yearly. You can cancel any time in the app, and your data is never deleted if you stop.

Is my data private?

Your logs are yours. We don't sell your data or use it for ads, and only a coach you choose to connect can see it. You can export or delete everything from inside the app. Read the Privacy Policy.

I'm a coach. What do my clients pay?

Nothing to FOREVER. Clients connected to a coach on an active plan use the app free; you charge them the way you always have. Your plan is priced by the number of clients you coach.
